# About this project

[中文 → ABOUT.zh-CN.md](ABOUT.zh-CN.md)

This tool is maintained by an independent market researcher, built to solve my own
day-to-day survey analysis work — not a commercial product. It's free and open for
anyone doing similar work.

**What it does**: turns raw survey exports into charts, cross-tabs, AI-assisted
open-ended answer coding, and one-click Word / PDF / Markdown reports. AI is only
used where semantic judgement is genuinely needed (translation, classification,
insights) — it never touches or invents numbers; every statistic is computed by
Python.

**What it doesn't do**: questionnaire design, sample recruitment, or advanced
statistical modeling (weighting, significance testing). It's a reporting/analysis
tool, not a full research platform.

If you're also working on AI + market research / branding and want to compare
notes, say hi in [Discussions](../../discussions) — always happy to meet people in
the same space.
